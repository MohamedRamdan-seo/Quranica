A STEP-BY-STEP APPROACH

Your Arabic learning roadmap.

Four distinct stages based on the content of Quranica’s published Learn Arabic course. Expand a level to explore topics, activities and its learning direction.

4
LEVELS
01LEVEL 01 · Start with letters, sounds and simple words.Arabic FoundationsDesigned for learners starting from the beginning.+
WHAT YOU WILL STUDY
Letters & pronunciation

Recognize Arabic letter shapes and learn how the sounds are pronounced.

Listening foundations

Practice understanding simple spoken words and familiar phrases.

Your first written words

Form Arabic letters, write words such as بيت (house), and move into short sentences.

Learning activities

  • Recognize and pronounce letter shapes
  • Listen to and repeat simple everyday vocabulary
  • Practice writing short words and sentences

Learning direction

Strengthen the essentials needed to begin reading and writing Arabic.

02LEVEL 02 · Grow in listening, reading, writing and speaking.The Four Language SkillsThe original course describes this level for learners aged 9+ who can already read and write Arabic.+
WHAT YOU WILL STUDY
Understand spoken Arabic

Work with everyday conversations, stories and other listening material.

Reading for meaning

Read stories and articles while practicing comprehension, pronunciation and expression.

Writing beyond single words

Build complete sentences, short paragraphs and simple stories.

Speaking in everyday situations

Practice introductions, conversations about family and familiar daily topics.

Learning activities

  • Listen to spoken Arabic with guided comprehension
  • Read passages and discuss their meaning
  • Write short paragraphs and practice conversations

Learning direction

Become more comfortable using all four skills in familiar situations.

03LEVEL 03 · Expand vocabulary and express ideas with more depth.Intermediate ArabicFor learners with a working foundation in conversational Arabic.+
WHAT YOU WILL STUDY
Richer vocabulary

Explore language about culture, society and contemporary topics.

Grammar development

Practice verb conjugation, noun declension and more detailed sentence structures.

Writing and expression

Develop essays, article-style passages and written opinions.

Discussion & summary

Discuss ideas and summarize texts to build fluency and understanding.

Learning activities

  • Apply grammar in meaningful sentences
  • Build vocabulary through discussions
  • Write longer passages and summaries

Learning direction

Develop stronger grammar, greater range and more fluent expression.

04LEVEL 04 · Explore nuanced language, complex texts and discussion.Advanced ArabicThe source course describes this level for learners aged 15+ with a strong Arabic foundation.+
WHAT YOU WILL STUDY
Advanced grammar & morphology

Study detailed Arabic structures and word formation.

Complex reading & analysis

Analyze literature, current articles and more demanding texts.

Advanced writing

Write essays, reports and creative work with an emphasis on clarity and style.

Discussion and interpretation

Explore cultural and social topics through longer conversations.

Learning activities

  • Analyze complex texts and language choices
  • Develop clear, sophisticated writing
  • Practice in-depth discussions and nuanced expression

Learning direction

Work toward more accurate and sophisticated use of Arabic across skills.

COURSE FAQ

Before you start learning Arabic.

Answers sourced from Quranica’s published course information; all prototype price details are marked clearly.

Do I need prior Arabic knowledge?+

No. The course begins with the Arabic alphabet, sounds and simple vocabulary at Level 1.

Are Arabic lessons live or recorded?+

The official course describes live online lessons with real-time interaction with native Arabic-speaking teachers.

What will I need to take classes?+

The published course FAQ recommends a computer or tablet and a stable internet connection. Digital textbooks, audio and interactive materials are provided.

Can I reschedule a lesson?+

The official Quranica FAQ says rescheduling is possible with advance notice, subject to tutor availability.

Is the $49/month price official?+

No. The $49 monthly example, 3–6 month duration, two weekly lessons and 45-minute lessons are illustrative placeholders in this design prototype.

How can I start?+

Use Book a Free Trial to register on the official Quranica website, or visit the official Arabic course page to check the current purchase options.
